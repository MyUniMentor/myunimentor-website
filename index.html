Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Call or WhatsApp +91 74175 57070.

What is MyUniMentor?

MyUniMentor is a career counselling and admissions guidance organisation based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. It helps Class 11–12 students choose the right stream, course and university, runs free career seminars for schools, and organises the School Career Guidance Summit, where universities meet students inside their schools.

Is career counselling at MyUniMentor free for students?

Yes. The first one-on-one counselling session is free for Class 11–12 students. Book through the form on this website, or call or WhatsApp +91 74175 57070.

Are MyUniMentor's career seminars free for schools?

Yes. Career guidance seminars for Class 11–12 students are offered free of cost to schools.

Which cities does MyUniMentor work in?

MyUniMentor works with schools in Dehradun (Uttarakhand) and Saharanpur (Uttar Pradesh), and counsels students across India online and offline. In 2027, we plan to expand to Rishikesh, Roorkee, Srinagar and the wider Garhwal region.

What is the School Career Guidance Summit?

It is an in-school career fair organised by MyUniMentor. Universities set up stalls on the school campus and Class 11–12 students meet them, ask questions and compare options without leaving the school campus. The first edition (October 2026) ran across 12 schools in Dehradun and Saharanpur in October 2026.

How can a university partner with MyUniMentor?

Universities can exhibit at the School Career Guidance Summit and use MyUniMentor's admission and outreach support. 29 universities are currently partners. Send an enquiry through the partnership form or email info@myunimentor.in.
