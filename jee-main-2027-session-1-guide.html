JEE Main 2027 Session 1: dates, pattern and a checklist

Registration for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 closes on 22 November 2026. If you are in Class 12 and plan to study engineering, architecture or planning, here is what the official NTA documents say about the dates, the paper and the application, in one place.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 20 Nov 2026·6 min read

JEE Main 2027 at a glance

JEE Main is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). It is the entrance exam for B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch and B.Planning at NITs, IIITs and many other institutes, and it is also the first step towards JEE Advanced for the IITs. In 2027 it is held in two sessions, January and April. You do not have to take both. If you appear in both, NTA considers the better of your two scores.

All dates below are from the JEE Main 2027 Information Bulletin and the public notice of 24 October 2026 on jeemain.nta.nic.in.

Session 1 dates

StepDate
Online application24 October to 22 November 2026 (up to 11:50 pm)
Last date for fee payment22 November 2026 (up to 11:50 pm)
Application with late fee23 to 26 November 2026 (up to 11:50 pm)
Correction window27 to 29 November 2026 (up to 11:50 pm)

Exam dates20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027

The exam runs in two shifts: 9 am to 12 noon and 3 pm to 6 pm. Your shift and date are given on your admit card.

For Session 2, the bulletin gives a tentative application window of 5 January to 3 February 2027 and exam dates of 2 to 7 April 2027. NTA will publish a separate notice for Session 2, and you can apply with the same application number.

What the paper looks like

JEE Main has separate papers for each course. Most students take Paper 1.

PaperForWhat it has
Paper 1B.E./B.TechMathematics, Physics and Chemistry. Each subject has 20 multiple-choice questions (Section A) and 5 numerical questions (Section B). 75 questions, 300 marks, 3 hours, computer-based.
Paper 2AB.ArchMathematics, Aptitude Test and a Drawing Test. Maths and Aptitude are computer-based; Drawing is on paper, on an A4 drawing sheet. 400 marks.
Paper 2BB.PlanningMathematics, Aptitude Test and planning-based questions, all computer-based. 100 questions, 400 marks.

Marking: a correct answer gets +4 and a wrong answer gets –1, in both the multiple-choice and the numerical sections. Unanswered questions get 0. Numerical answers are rounded off to the nearest integer. So a careless guess in the numerical section costs you a mark too.

In Paper 1, if two students have the same total score, NTA breaks the tie using the Mathematics score first, then Physics, then Chemistry.

The paper is available in 13 languages: English, Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.

Before you apply: a checklist

  1. Sign in through MeriPehchaan. Registration uses the MeriPehchaan single sign-on, which works with DigiLocker, JanParichay and e-Pramaan. Identity is checked by eKYC using Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or APAAR.
  2. Check your details. Make sure your name, date of birth and other details in the form are correct before you submit. Check spellings carefully.
  3. Get your photo right. The bulletin asks for a recent colour photo in JPG/JPEG, between 10 kB and 200 kB, with 80% of the face visible (including ears, without a mask) against a white background. A live photo is also taken while filling the form.
  4. Scan the rest. Signature in JPG/JPEG between 10 kB and 100 kB. Class 10 certificate and, if applicable, disability certificate as PDF between 50 kB and 300 kB.
  5. Choose four cities with care. You can pick up to four exam cities from the list in the bulletin. Pick places you can reach easily early on exam day.
  6. Submit only one form. NTA warns that multiple forms can lead to strict action. If you need to start again, NTA allows a fresh form for Session 1 before the deadline, but only the last form with a successful fee payment counts, and the earlier fee is not refunded.
  7. Save your confirmation page. You don't need to post any documents to NTA. Keep a printout and a PDF of the confirmation page.
MyUniMentor tip

Don't wait for the last evening. Payment gateways and the portal slow down close to a deadline. Finish your form and payment a few days early, then use the correction window (27–29 November) only if you spot a mistake. NTA lists which fields can be corrected when the window opens.

How to use the time until January

  • Plan around your boards. Session 1 falls in the middle of your Class 12 board preparation. Keep revising board topics alongside JEE practice; much of the syllabus overlaps.
  • Practise in the real format. Three hours on a computer is different from a paper test. Take timed mock tests and practise the numerical section separately.
  • Watch accuracy. With –1 for every wrong answer, skipping a question you can't narrow down is often the better choice.
  • Treat Session 1 as a real attempt. If it goes well, you have more time for boards. If not, you still have Session 2 in April.

Think beyond the exam

What is the last date to apply for JEE Main 2027 Session 1?

The last date is 22 November 2026, up to 11:50 pm, including fee payment. Applications with a late fee are accepted from 23 to 26 November 2026.

When is JEE Main 2027 Session 1?

Session 1 is on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027, in two shifts: 9 am to 12 noon and 3 pm to 6 pm. Admit cards are due on 13 January 2027.

Do I need to take both sessions of JEE Main?

No. You can take one or both. If you take both, NTA uses the better of your two JEE Main 2027 scores.

Is there negative marking in JEE Main 2027?

Yes. Each correct answer gets +4 and each wrong answer gets –1, in both the multiple-choice and the numerical sections. Unanswered questions get 0.
