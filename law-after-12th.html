Law (BA LLB) after 12th

You can start studying law right after Class 12 through a five-year integrated degree, such as BA LLB or BBA LLB. It combines a bachelor's degree with a law degree and prepares you to practise as a lawyer.

Duration
5 years (integrated)
Stream
Any stream
Entrance
CLAT, AILET or university tests
Next step
Practice, corporate law or LLM

What you study in Law (BA LLB)

  • Constitutional law and the Indian legal system
  • Contracts, torts and criminal law
  • Corporate and commercial law
  • Property, family and labour law
  • Moot courts, legal research and writing
  • Internships with lawyers, firms and courts

Who Law (BA LLB) suits

Law (BA LLB) is a good fit if you:

  • Enjoy reading and arguing a point
  • Are curious about rules, rights and justice
  • Write and speak clearly
  • Can read long texts with focus

Specialisations

Choose BA LLB if you are drawn to subjects like political science and history. Choose BBA LLB if you are interested in business and corporate law. Both lead to the same LLB qualification.

Entrance exams and eligibility

CLAT is the main exam for National Law Universities. CLAT 2027 has 120 multiple-choice questions in 2 hours across five sections: English, current affairs, legal reasoning, logical reasoning and quantitative techniques. Wrong answers cost 0.25 marks. NLU Delhi uses AILET, and many universities run their own tests. See the exam calendar.

Careers after Law (BA LLB)

  • Litigation in courts
  • Corporate lawyer in a law firm or company
  • Judicial services (judge)
  • Legal advisor or in-house counsel
  • Policy, research and public interest law
  • Legal journalism or academics

Can a Science or Commerce student study law?

Yes. Integrated law programmes are open to students from all streams.

Do I need to know law before CLAT?

No. CLAT's legal reasoning section gives you the rules in the passage. It tests reasoning and reading, not prior legal knowledge.

What is the difference between BA LLB and LLB?

BA LLB is a five-year integrated degree you start after Class 12. LLB is a three-year degree you can take after completing any graduation.
