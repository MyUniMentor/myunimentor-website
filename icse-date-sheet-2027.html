ICSE Class 10 timetable 2027: every paper, date and timing

CISCE’s ICSE (Class 10) exams 2027 begin on Monday, 15 February with English Language and end on Wednesday, 31 March. Below is every paper with its date and time, from CISCE’s official timetable of 10 November 2026.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 24 Nov 2026·5 min read

Key facts

Monday, 15 February 2027 (English Language)
Wednesday, 31 March 2027 (Economics, French and Sanskrit as Group II electives)
Most papers at 10:15 am; Art papers at 9:00 am
Be seated by 9:45 am for 10:15 am papers, and by 8:30 am for 9:00 am papers
15 minutes, in addition to the writing time
April or May 2027, on cisce.org
10 November 2026, by CISCE

Every date on this page is taken from CISCE’s official “ICSE (Class X) Year 2027 Examination Timetable and Instructions for Candidates”, available on cisce.org.

MyUniMentor tip

The three Science papers come one after another in March, with three to four days between them. Finish your first full revision of all three by the end of February, and use the gaps for past papers. Results, recheck and re-evaluation

CISCE will declare the ICSE 2027 results in April or May 2027 on cisce.org. A recheck request must reach CISCE within four days of the results, and re-evaluation can be requested within three days of the recheck results. You can also take an improvement exam in up to three subjects.

When does the ICSE 2027 exam start?

On Monday, 15 February 2027, with English Language (English Paper 1) at 10:15 am.

When is the ICSE 2027 Maths exam?

Mathematics is on Monday, 22 February 2027, at 10:15 am, for 3 hours.

When are the ICSE 2027 Science papers?

Physics is on 8 March, Chemistry on 12 March and Biology on 15 March 2027, each at 10:15 am for 2 hours.

What time should I reach for ICSE exams?

You must be seated by 9:45 am for papers that start at 10:15 am, and by 8:30 am for Art papers that start at 9:00 am.

When will ICSE 2027 results be declared?

CISCE says the ICSE 2027 results will be declared in April or May 2027 on cisce.org.
