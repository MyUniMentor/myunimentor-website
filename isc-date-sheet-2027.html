ISC Class 12 timetable 2027: every paper, date and timing

CISCE’s ISC (Class 12) exams 2027 begin on Tuesday, 9 February and end on Thursday, 8 April. Below is every paper with its date and time, from CISCE’s official timetable of 10 November 2026, along with reporting times, what you can take into the hall, and how rechecks work.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 24 Nov 2026·6 min read

Key facts

Tuesday, 9 February 2027 (Environmental Science, Western Music)
Thursday, 8 April 2027 (Legal Studies)
Most papers at 2:00 pm; Art papers at 9:00 am
Be seated by 1:30 pm for 2:00 pm papers, and by 8:30 am for 9:00 am papers
15 minutes, in addition to the writing time
3 hours for every paper
April or May 2027, on cisce.org
10 November 2026, by CISCE

Every date on this page is taken from CISCE’s official “ISC (Class XII) Year 2027 Examination Timetable and Instructions for Candidates”, available on cisce.org.

MyUniMentor tip

ISC starts a week before CBSE, and most papers are in the afternoon. Write your mock papers at 2 pm, not in the morning, so you are used to the timing. Physics, Biology and Computer Science all fall in March, so finish your first revision of them by the end of February.

What you can and cannot take into the hall

  • Allowed: your CISCE admission card, blue or black pens, pencils, scale, eraser, sharpener, colour pencils, drawing instruments, and a calculator of the type CISCE allows (Casio fx-82 MS or similar, or a basic calculator).
  • Not allowed: books or notes, mobile phones, smart watches, earbuds, AI or smart glasses, smart rings, smart or scanner pens, wallets and cash.

Results, recheck and re-evaluation

CISCE will declare the ISC 2027 results in April or May 2027 on cisce.org. If you are not satisfied, you can apply online for a recheck; the request must reach CISCE within four days of the results. After the recheck results, you can apply for re-evaluation within three days, only in the subjects you asked to recheck. Both are paid services. You can also take an improvement exam in up to three subjects in the same year.

When does the ISC 2027 exam start?

On Tuesday, 9 February 2027, with Environmental Science and Western Music Paper 1. Most papers start at 2:00 pm.

When is the ISC 2027 English exam?

English Paper 1 (English Language) is on Wednesday, 10 February 2027, and English Paper 2 (Literature in English) on Monday, 15 February 2027.

When are ISC 2027 Physics, Chemistry and Maths?

Chemistry Paper 1 is on 24 February, Mathematics on 1 March and Physics Paper 1 on 8 March 2027, all at 2:00 pm.

What time should I reach for ISC exams?

You must be seated by 1:30 pm for papers that start at 2:00 pm, and by 8:30 am for Art papers that start at 9:00 am. Reading time is an extra 15 minutes.

When will ISC 2027 results be declared?

CISCE says the ISC 2027 results will be declared in April or May 2027 on cisce.org.
