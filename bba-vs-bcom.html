BBA vs B.Com: which one fits you?

BBA and B.Com are two of the most popular courses after Class 12, especially for Commerce students. They sound similar, but they train you for different kinds of work. Here's how to tell which one fits you.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 20 Nov 2026·6 min read

The short answer

B.Com goes deep into accounting, finance, taxation and commerce. It suits students who like numbers and want careers like Chartered Accountancy or finance.

BBA is about how organisations are run: management, marketing, people and strategy. It suits students who want to manage teams, start a business, or move into an MBA.

Side by side

BBAB.Com
FocusManagement, marketing, HR, operations, entrepreneurshipAccounting, finance, taxation, economics, business law
Style of learningCase studies, presentations, projects, internshipsConcepts, numerical problems, theory
Duration3 years (4 years with honours at many universities under NEP)3 years (4 years with honours at many universities under NEP)
StreamAny streamCommerce is preferred; some universities accept other streams
Natural next stepMBA, or a job in sales, marketing, HR or operationsCA, CS, CMA, M.Com, MBA (Finance), or finance and accounts roles

Choose BBA if you…

  • Enjoy leading groups, presenting and convincing people
  • Want to run your own business or a family business one day
  • Are planning an MBA and want a management foundation
  • Prefer practical, project-based learning over heavy theory

Choose B.Com if you…

  • Enjoy Accountancy and are comfortable with numbers
  • Are planning CA, CS or CMA alongside or after your degree
  • Want a career in banking, taxation, audit or financial analysis
  • Like understanding exactly how money moves through a business
MyUniMentor tip

Don't choose on the name of the degree alone. Look at the specialisations a university offers, such as BBA in Business Analytics or B.Com in FinTech. The specialisation often matters more than the degree title.

Admissions and entrance exams

Many universities admit BBA and B.Com students through CUET UG. Some run their own tests or admit on Class 12 marks. If you are aiming for a five-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) at an IIM, you'll need IPMAT or JIPMAT. See our exam calendar for dates.

Does one pay more than the other?

Starting roles depend far more on your college, internships, skills and specialisation than on the degree name. A B.Com graduate who clears CA, and a BBA graduate who goes on to a strong MBA, can both build excellent careers. Choose the path you'll enjoy working hard at.

Can a Science student do BBA or B.Com?

Yes. BBA is open to all streams at most universities. Many universities also accept Science students for B.Com, though some prefer Commerce. Check each university's eligibility.

Is Maths required for BBA?

Most universities do not require Maths for BBA. Some business analytics or finance specialisations and the IPMAT for IIM programmes expect Maths. Check the course you're targeting.

Which is better for CA, BBA or B.Com?

B.Com overlaps much more with the CA syllabus, which makes it the more common choice for CA aspirants.

Is BBA good for an MBA?

Yes. BBA gives a strong management foundation for an MBA, but MBA admissions are open to graduates of any discipline.
