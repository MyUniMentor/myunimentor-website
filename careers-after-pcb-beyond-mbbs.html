Careers after PCB beyond MBBS

For many PCB students, MBBS feels like the only option. It isn't. Biology opens doors to dozens of healthcare, science and research careers, many of them in high demand. Here are twelve worth knowing.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 20 Nov 2026·7 min read

Through NEET UG

These courses usually admit students through NEET UG, the same exam used for MBBS.

  • BDS (Dentistry): five years including internship. Graduates practise as dentists or specialise further.
  • BAMS, BHMS, BUMS (AYUSH): degrees in Ayurveda, Homoeopathy and Unani medicine, with clinical practice and hospital roles.
  • BVSc and AH (Veterinary Science): treat animals, work in livestock, wildlife or public health. Many veterinary seats are filled through NEET.
  • B.Sc Nursing: many institutes use NEET or their own entrance test. Nurses are in demand in India and abroad.

Healthcare without NEET at many universities

  • Physiotherapy (BPT): help patients recover movement after injury or surgery. Strong demand in hospitals, sports and clinics.
  • Pharmacy (B.Pharm): medicines, drug development and the pharma industry. Open to PCB and PCM students.
  • Allied Health Sciences: Medical Lab Technology, Radiology and Imaging, Optometry, Operation Theatre Technology and more. These are essential hospital roles.
  • Nutrition and Dietetics: work in hospitals, sports, wellness and food industries.
MyUniMentor tip

Admission rules vary by university and state. Some allied health courses accept Class 12 marks; others use NEET or their own test. Always check eligibility on the university's admission page.

Science and research

  • Biotechnology and Microbiology: labs, research, pharma and agriculture. A good fit for students who enjoy experiments.
  • BS-MS at IISERs: research-focused programmes in the sciences. Admission is through the IISER Aptitude Test.
  • Forensic Science: scientific analysis for investigations, in labs and government agencies.

Mind and society

  • Psychology: understanding behaviour and mental health. A path to counselling and clinical psychology with further study.

How to choose

Think about what drew you to Biology. If it was helping people, look at physiotherapy, nursing, allied health or psychology. If it was curiosity about how life works, look at biotechnology or research. If you want to treat patients as a doctor, BDS and AYUSH are strong paths through NEET.

What can I do after PCB if I don't get MBBS?

BDS, AYUSH courses, veterinary science, nursing, physiotherapy, pharmacy, allied health sciences, biotechnology, nutrition, forensic science, psychology and research programmes are all strong options.

Which PCB courses don't need NEET?

Many universities admit students to physiotherapy, pharmacy, allied health, biotechnology, psychology and nutrition without NEET. Rules vary by university and state, so check eligibility for each.

Is BDS a good option?

Yes, for students who want to practise as clinicians. BDS is a five-year programme including internship, and dentists can practise or specialise further.

Can PCB students do engineering?

Most B.Tech programmes require Maths in Class 12. Some universities offer Biotechnology or related programmes to PCB students. Check each university's eligibility.
