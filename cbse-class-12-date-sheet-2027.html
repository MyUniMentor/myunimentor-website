CBSE Class 12 date sheet 2027: every subject, date and timing

CBSE’s Class 12 board exams 2027 begin on Tuesday, 16 February with Physics and end on 9 April. Below are all 121 subjects with their dates and timings, copied from CBSE’s official date sheet of 6 November 2026, plus the gaps between papers for each stream.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 24 Nov 2026·6 min read

Key facts

Tuesday, 16 February 2027 (Physics)
Friday, 9 April 2027 (Retail, Web Application)
All papers start at 10:30 am IST
121 subjects in Class 12
Over 19.8 lakh in Class 12
6 November 2026, 101 days before the exams
cbse.gov.in, Examination circulars: “Date Sheet for Class X and Class XII Board Examinations, 2027”

Every date on this page is taken from CBSE’s official date sheet, dated 6 November 2026. CBSE says it planned the schedule so that no two subjects a student has chosen fall on the same day, and so that the boards finish before the major entrance exams.

Stream-wise schedule and preparation gaps

How many days you get between papers matters more than the dates themselves. Here are the common subject combinations, with the gap before each paper.

Science (PCM)

  1. Tue, 16 Feb Physics
  2. Sat, 27 Feb Chemistry 11 days after the previous paper
  3. Fri, 5 Mar English Core 6 days after the previous paper
  4. Tue, 9 Mar Mathematics 4 days after the previous paper

Science (PCB)

  1. Tue, 16 Feb Physics
  2. Sat, 27 Feb Chemistry 11 days after the previous paper
  3. Fri, 5 Mar English Core 6 days after the previous paper
  4. Thu, 25 Mar Biology 20 days after the previous paper

Commerce with Maths

  1. Tue, 23 Feb Accountancy
  2. Fri, 5 Mar English Core 10 days after the previous paper
  3. Tue, 9 Mar Mathematics 4 days after the previous paper
  4. Tue, 16 Mar Economics 7 days after the previous paper
  5. Thu, 1 Apr Business Studies 16 days after the previous paper

Humanities

  1. Thu, 25 Feb Geography
  2. Tue, 2 Mar Psychology 5 days after the previous paper

  4. Tue, 16 Mar Economics 11 days after the previous paper
  5. Fri, 19 Mar Political Science 3 days after the previous paper

MyUniMentor tip

Use the long gaps for your hardest subjects. In PCM, the 11 days between Physics and Chemistry are your biggest block, and Mathematics comes 4 days after English; in PCB, you get 20 days between English and Biology. Plan backwards from each date, and keep the last two days before every paper for past papers only.

When does the CBSE Class 12 exam 2027 start?

On Tuesday, 16 February 2027, with Physics (042). All papers start at 10:30 am IST.

When is the CBSE Class 12 Maths exam 2027?

Mathematics (041) and Applied Mathematics (241) are on Tuesday, 9 March 2027, from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm.

When are Chemistry and Biology in CBSE Class 12 2027?

Chemistry (043) is on Saturday, 27 February 2027, and Biology (044) is on Thursday, 25 March 2027.

When is the English exam for CBSE Class 12 2027?

English Core (301) and English Elective (001) are on Friday, 5 March 2027.

When is the last CBSE Class 12 exam in 2027?

The last papers are Retail (801) and Web Application (803) on Friday, 9 April 2027. Among the main subjects, Legal Studies (074) is on 8 April and Business Studies (054) on 1 April.

When will CBSE Class 12 results 2027 come out?

CBSE has not announced the date. For reference, it declared the 2026 Class 12 results on 13 May 2026.
