School Career Guidance Summit

The first edition ran in October 2026 across 12 schools. An in-school career fair. Universities set up stalls on the school campus and Class 11–12 students meet them, ask questions and compare options — without leaving the school campus.

The people behind the stalls

Admissions teams from our partner universities with the MyUniMentor team, at the wrap-up of the 2026 edition at Saigrace Academy International, Dehradun.

A career fair, inside the school

Most career fairs ask students to travel to a city hall. The Career Summit comes to them — so every Class 11–12 student in the school gets to meet universities in person.

  • Partner universities set up stalls on the school campus
  • Each university's own delegates give first-hand information on courses, fees and admissions
  • Students get their doubts cleared on the spot, one-to-one
  • Parents and teachers can join in and ask questions too
  • Completed within 2.5 hours, including setup
  • The first edition ran in October 2026 across Dehradun and Saharanpur
