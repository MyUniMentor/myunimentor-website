12 questions to ask a university before you apply

Every university brochure looks impressive. The real difference shows up when you ask the right questions. Use this list at university stalls, on campus visits and on admission calls before you pay anything.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 20 Nov 2026·7 min read

Recognition and quality

  1. Is the university recognised by the UGC? You can check the official list on the UGC website. For engineering and management, also ask whether the programme is approved by AICTE where it applies.
  2. What is its NAAC grade, and is it current? NAAC accreditation is valid for a fixed period. Ask for the grade and the year it was awarded.
  3. Does it appear in the NIRF rankings? NIRF is the Government of India's ranking framework. Check the category relevant to your course.

Placements and outcomes

  1. What percentage of students in my course were placed last year? Ask for your specific programme, not the whole university.
  2. What was the median package, not just the highest? The highest package is one student. The median tells you what a typical student gets.
  3. Which companies hired from my course, and for which roles? Ask for a list from the last two years.
  4. Are internships part of the programme? Ask how the university helps students find them.
MyUniMentor tip

Ask to speak to a current student or a recent graduate from your course. Ten minutes with them will tell you more than any brochure.

Teaching and campus

  1. Who teaches the core subjects? Ask about faculty qualifications and industry experience in your department.
  2. What is the hostel like, and is it guaranteed? Ask about rooms, food, safety, curfew rules and whether first-year students get a hostel seat.

Money

  1. What is the total cost for the full course? Ask for tuition plus hostel, mess, exam fees, uniforms, laptops and any one-time charges, for every year.
  2. What scholarships are available, and what do I need to keep them? Many scholarships need a minimum score every semester. Get the conditions in writing.
  3. What is the refund policy if I withdraw? The UGC has issued guidelines on fee refunds. Ask how the university applies them before you pay.

How to use this list

Take the same questions to every university on your shortlist and write the answers side by side. The best choice is usually clear once you compare honestly. A MyUniMentor counsellor can help you build and compare your shortlist.

How do I check if a university is recognised?

The University Grants Commission (UGC) publishes the list of recognised universities on its official website. Check that the university appears there before applying.

Is the highest placement package a good measure?

Not on its own. The highest package is usually one student. Ask for the median package and the percentage of students placed in your specific course.

What costs should I ask about besides tuition?

Hostel, mess, exam fees, registration, uniforms, laptops, transport and any one-time charges. Ask for the total cost for the full course, year by year.

Can I get my fees back if I change my mind?

The UGC has issued guidelines on fee refunds when students withdraw. Ask the university how it applies them and get the refund policy in writing before paying.
