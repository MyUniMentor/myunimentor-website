About Om Garg

When Om was in school, there was no one to explain where a course actually leads. He had to research everything on his own.

That experience shaped his work. Om spent five years in a senior cross-functional role in the education sector, guiding students and speaking at schools, before founding MyUniMentor in Dehradun in 2026.

Today he leads MyUniMentor's mission of bringing honest, expert career guidance to every student in India, through one-on-one counselling, free tools and guides, and sessions in schools.
