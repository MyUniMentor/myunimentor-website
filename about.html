Career guidance for every student

Our mission is simple: every student in India should get honest, expert guidance before choosing a stream, a course or a university, wherever they live and whichever school they go to.

Why we exist

For most students in India, the biggest decision of their life is made on hearsay: a relative's advice, a coaching brochure, what a friend is picking.

Real career guidance has always been a privilege. Some students get a counsellor, a career fair and someone to explain where a course actually leads. Most never do.

MyUniMentor exists to change that. We want guidance to reach every single student, through one-on-one counselling, free tools anyone can use, and sessions in schools and communities.

What we believe

  1. 1

    Every student, not a few

    Good guidance shouldn't depend on your city, your school or who your family knows.

  2. 2

    Fit over hype

    The right course is the one that fits you, not the one everyone around you is picking.

  3. 3

    Clarity before deadlines

    Decisions made early and calmly are better than decisions made the night before a form closes.
