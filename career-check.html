About the career check

Still unsure? Call or WhatsApp +91 74175 57070.

Is the MyUniMentor career check free?

Yes. It is free and takes about four minutes. You share your name and phone number before you see your result, and a MyUniMentor counsellor calls you to talk it through.

Who is it for?

Students in Class 9, 10, 11 and 12. Class 9 and 10 students also see which stream fits each career path.

How is it different from other career tests?

The questions change with your stream and your answers. Commerce, PCM, PCB, PCMB and Humanities students each get their own set of activities, and the check then asks follow-up questions only about your top areas.

Is this a psychometric test?

No. It is a short guidance tool based on your interests, stream and plans. For a detailed plan, a counsellor looks at your marks, strengths and options with you.
