CBSE Class 10 date sheet 2027: every subject, date and timing

CBSE’s Class 10 board exams 2027 begin on 16 February, with Mathematics on 17 February, and end on 9 March. Below are all 83 subjects with their dates and timings, copied from CBSE’s official date sheet of 6 November 2026.

By Om Garg, Founder, MyUniMentor·Updated 24 Nov 2026·5 min read

Key facts

Tuesday, 16 February 2027 (Painting)
Wednesday, 17 February 2027 (Mathematics Standard and Basic)
Tuesday, 9 March 2027 (languages, NCC, Book Keeping & Accountancy)

The five core subjects

  1. Wed, 17 Feb Mathematics Standard
  2. Mon, 22 Feb Science 5 days after the previous paper
  3. Fri, 26 Feb English (Language and Literature) 4 days after the previous paper
  4. Wed, 3 Mar Hindi Course-A 5 days after the previous paper
  5. Mon, 8 Mar Social Science 5 days after the previous paper
MyUniMentor tip

Mathematics comes first, on 17 February, so start full-length practice papers in December. After that, the five core papers are four to five days apart, which is enough for one subject at a time if your first revision is done by early February. Class 10 is also when you choose your stream. When does the CBSE Class 10 exam 2027 start?

The first paper is Painting (049) on Tuesday, 16 February 2027. The main subjects begin with Mathematics Standard (041) and Basic (241) on Wednesday, 17 February 2027.

When is the CBSE Class 10 Science exam 2027?

Science (086) is on Monday, 22 February 2027, from 10:30 am to 1:30 pm.

When is the CBSE Class 10 Social Science exam 2027?

Social Science (087) is on Monday, 8 March 2027.

When is the CBSE Class 10 English exam 2027?

English Language and Literature (184) and English Communicative (101) are on Friday, 26 February 2027.

When is the last CBSE Class 10 exam in 2027?

Tuesday, 9 March 2027, with several languages, NCC and Elements of Book Keeping & Accountancy.
