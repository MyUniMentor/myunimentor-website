Career guidance for schools

Free career seminars and the School Career Guidance Summit — organised career guidance for your Class 11–12 students, on your own campus.

Bring career guidance to your campus

We run career guidance seminars for Class 11–12 students at no cost to the school, and bring universities to your campus through the School Career Guidance Summit.

  • Career guidance seminars for Class 11–12, free for schools
  • The School Career Guidance Summit on your campus
  • Universities set up stalls; students meet them without leaving the school campus
  • Planned around your timetable, within 2.5 hours
  • Your school provides the stage and AV; we handle the rest

How it works

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Send an enquiry or call us to pick a date.

  2. 2

    We plan it with you

    Session format, timing and student groups, around your timetable.

  3. 3

    On the day

    Our team runs the seminar or the summit on your campus.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    Students who want one-on-one guidance can book a counselling session.
